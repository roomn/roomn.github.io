Frequently Asked Questions

Why does macOS say the app is from an "unidentified developer"?

Roomn apps are independently built and distributed without a paid Apple Developer certificate. On first launch, macOS may block the app. To open it safely: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

Are Roomn apps truly free?

Yes. All core features of Roomn apps are free to download and use without mandatory logins or hidden subscriptions. Voluntary support helps keep future updates coming.
